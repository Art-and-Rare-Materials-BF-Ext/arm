Art & Rare Materials BIBFRAME Ontology v1.0 - Relator Vocabulary

Created using SKOS Play

  • Accessioner
    • DEF : The act of adding a resource to a collection.
  • Acquisition
    • DEF : The act of gaining possession of a resource.
  • Award granter
    • DEF : The granter/provider of an award.
  • Award selector
    • DEF : The act of nominating or judging a resource in relation to an award, honor, etc.
  • Borrower
    • DEF : The act of taking possession, but not ownership, of a resource.
  • Buyer
    • DEF : The act of purchasing a resource.
  • Claimant
    • DEF : The act of declaring ownership of a resource, notably when the claim is not clearly substantiated.
  • Deaccessioner
    • DEF : The act of permanently removing accessioned items from a collection or repository.
  • Dealer
    • DEF : The act of negotiating or arranging for a transaction.
  • Destroyer
    • DEF : The act of permanently destroying a resource.
  • Discarder
    • DEF : The act of permanently removing a resource or part of a resource from a collection for disposal.
  • Disputant
    • DEF : The act of challenging a claim, such as a claim of ownership.
  • Exhibitor
    • DEF : The act of showing or presenting a resource for public view.
  • Inheritor
    • DEF : The act of taking possession of a resource upon the death of the previous owner, either through bequest or application of law.
  • Loss
    • DEF : Responsibility for the disappearance of a resource where the circumstances of the disappearance are unknown.
  • Offer
    • DEF : The act of providing a resource for purchase or other form of acquisition. Use for booksellers and sellers of other resources.
  • Recipient
    • DEF : The act of receiving a resource, via bequest, donation, etc.
  • Recoverer
    • DEF : The act of re-assuming ownership of a resource following a period of loss, possibly due to theft.
  • Repatriator
    • DEF : The act of returning a resource to its home country.
  • Testator
    • DEF : The act of bequeathing a resource in a will.
  • Thief
    • DEF : The act of acquiring a resource without the consent of the legal owner.
  • Transferer
    • DEF : The act of giving possession of a resource to another party, notably without a defined sale or donation.
  • Witness
    • DEF : The activity of verifying the truthfulness of an event or action.
