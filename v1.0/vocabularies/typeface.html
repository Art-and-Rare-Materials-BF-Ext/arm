  • gothic-antique
    • DEF : Subgroup of black letter typefaces which are a mix of black letter and roman; rounder and less angular than black letter, and most vertical strokes lack "feet" or ending strokes.
  • rotunda
    • DEF : Subgroup of black letter typefaces; basically the Southern European black letter.
  • textura
    • DEF : Subgroup of black letter typefaces which are squarely drawn and almost completely lacking curves.
